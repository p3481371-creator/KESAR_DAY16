# KESAR · Kashmiri saffron

Concept D2C website for a Kashmiri saffron brand. A 698-frame scroll film: a crocus opens at dawn in Pampore, three red threads are picked, they fall into a glass of milk, and the milk turns gold.

Features: saffron-thread cursor, word-by-word film captions, shop with grade/weight/subscription, rolling prices, orbiting threads, bag drawer, grade explainer, sideways harvest story, interactive water test, jar passport, ritual dosing with a steeping timer, gift note, falling-thread footer.

Day 16 adds fifteen interactive moments:
- Pick the three threads yourself: the film pauses on the open flower and you drag them out.
- Stir the golden milk with your cursor at the end of the film.
- The film is colour-graded to the time of day in Kashmir (tap to change).
- Optional sound made in the browser (off by default): wind, plucks, the pour.
- A brass scale that weighs your thread count.
- A wax seal stamps the jar when you add to bag.
- A downloadable harvest card after checkout.
- A loupe comparing real and fake threads (hold Shift to switch).
- A crocus that lives as long as your visit.
- A draggable 3D map of the Pampore villages.
- Papier-mâché patterns that paint themselves in the gift section.
- A wall of 1,50,000 dots, one per flower in a kilo.
- The Saffron Hour, every night 9 to 10 pm IST (preview from the footer).
- A gift that unwraps itself, with a shareable link.
- Type "kesar" anywhere.

Open `index.html` through any static server (the film loads from `pack/`).
